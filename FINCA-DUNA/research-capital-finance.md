# Capital Stack & Finance — Finca Duna / Sun Nation

**Why this matters:** An investor says "yes" only when the size of the ask, the structure of the capital, and the cost/revenue baseline are credible against real comparables. This node sizes the ask and shows the money can be structured to de-risk a multi-year coffee ramp.

---

## CORE QUESTION
How much capital does Finca Duna realistically need, in what structure, and how does that compare to actual specialty-coffee / regenerative-ag deals — for a farm-only path (Option A) vs. a platform path (Option B)?

## KEY SUB-QUESTIONS
1. What do comparable raises in specialty coffee / regenerative ag actually look like (size, stage, structure)?
2. What blended / catalytic / patient-capital instruments exist for smallholder & specialty coffee, and on what terms?
3. What are the cost baselines for a Colombian coffee farm (land, establishment/renovation, operating cost, revenue ramp)?
4. What is a defensible order-of-magnitude capital ask for Option A vs. Option B?

---

## DATAPOINTS GATHERED

### 1. Comparable raises / deal sizes & structures
- **Global agrifoodtech funding ~$16.0B (2024) and ~$16.2B (2025)**, roughly flat YoY; investors favoring "real unit economics" and clear revenue paths. *(Confirmed)* — AgFunder.
- **Developing-markets agrifoodtech funding surged ~63%** in the latest report, bucking the global decline. *(Confirmed, direction; exact $ not pulled)* — AgFunder.
- **Green Coffee Company (Colombia, vertically-integrated coffee farms):** ~**$63.2M raised across 4 rounds**; **$25M Series C equity (May 2024)**, **$15M round (Sept 2024)**, **$13.2M Series B**; **$100M Series C target** announced (not fully closed at that figure); separately planning a **~$35M wet-mill investment** to double processing by 2027. *(Confirmed)* — PR Newswire, World Coffee Portal, StoneX, Tracxn.
- **Root Capital loans range $200K–$2M**, repayment tied to harvest/sales cycles; example cooperative grew from a $300K loan to a $1.5M line of credit. *(Confirmed)* — Root Capital / Blended Finance Taskforce.
- **Starbucks Global Farmer Fund: $50M commitment** for farmer financing across Latin America. *(Confirmed)* — Starbucks.
- **IDB Invest into responsAbility Sustainable Food Fund – Latam I: up to $15M**, mostly structured/mezzanine debt to mid-sized producers (Mexico, Peru, Colombia, Chile). *(Confirmed)* — IDB Invest.
- **IDB Invest / Mercon coffee facility: up to $50M** sustainability-linked revolving credit, part of a **$500M syndicated line** across the coffee value chain. *(Confirmed)* — IDB Invest.

### 2. Blended / catalytic / patient-capital structures
- **Blended finance cumulative: ~$213B across 1,200+ deals** (Convergence, mid-2025). *(Confirmed)* — Convergence.
- **Common concessional instruments:** first-loss tranches, subordinated/mezzanine debt, guarantees & political-risk insurance, technical-assistance grants, results-based payments. *(Confirmed)* — Convergence / Sustainability Atlas.
- **Guarantees used in ~35% of blended deals (~$77B flow).** *(Confirmed)* — Convergence.
- **Renovation/tree-crop loan terms:** tenors often **10–15 years** (sometimes shorter); smallholders need **6–8 year** loans for profitability; **grace periods + flexible repayment** are standard to match the multi-year non-yield window. *(Confirmed)* — IDH / Dalberg / Root Capital.
- Providers to target: Root Capital, responsAbility, IDB Invest, IFC, IDH, MCE Social Capital (USAID INVEST-backed), Starbucks Global Farmer Fund. *(Confirmed as active in coffee/LatAm ag.)*

### 3. Cost baselines — Colombian coffee farm
- **Land:** very hard to quote per-hectare cleanly — usually sold as packages (land + farmhouse + processing). Reported **minimum farm entry ~COP 100M (~US$29,000, Apr 2024)**. *(Confirmed but coarse; SNSM-specific $/ha NOT found — gap.)* — BizLatin Hub.
- **Forest-to-ag conversion:** ~**US$200/ha** for deforestation/clearing; land non-yield-bearing for **3–5 years**. *(Confirmed; clearing only, excludes planting.)* — BizLatin Hub.
- **Renovation/replant:** ~**COP 8–12M/ha (~US$2,860–4,285/ha)**. *(Confirmed)* — BizLatin Hub.
- **Plantation metrics:** avg farm **4.5 ha**; tree density **~5,340/ha**; productivity **>14 bags(60kg)/ha**; Colombia replanted **~90,000 ha (~11% of area)** in 2024. *(Confirmed)* — USDA FAS GAIN / Daily Coffee News.
- **Farmgate price:** FNC buy rate **~COP 2,230,000/carga (125kg) ≈ US$526** late 2024 — historically high. *(Confirmed)* — Daily Coffee News.
- **Production economics:** Cornell/Fair Trade USA study — **Colombia was the only LatAm benchmark covering full depreciation** at prevailing farmgate prices (Peru +2c/lb, Mexico/Honduras +6c/lb needed). *(Confirmed; signals Colombia is comparatively viable.)* — Cornell / Daily Coffee News.
- **Revenue ramp (Arabica):** first harvest **year 3–4**; full production **year 6–8**; most productive **years 7–20**. *(Confirmed)* — multiple horticultural/NCA sources.

### 4. Capital-ask scaffold (ORDERS OF MAGNITUDE — assumptions stated; all *Estimated*)
**Assumptions:** ~30–50 ha target; SNSM specialty Arabica; mix of existing + renovated/new planting; land bought as a package; figures are planning ranges, not quotes.

**Option A — Simple Farm (farm only, sell green):**
- Land/farm package: **~$0.5M–$1.5M** (highly site-dependent; *biggest unknown*).
- Establishment/renovation: ~$3–4.3K/ha × 30–50 ha = **~$100K–$215K**.
- Operating runway through year 3–4 (pre-revenue ramp): **~$150K–$400K**.
- **Indicative total ask: ~$0.8M–$2.0M.** Structure: equity + patient debt with 3–4yr grace (Root Capital-style).

**Option B — Regenerative Coffee Platform (+ warehouse, processing, export, fund, regional purchasing):**
- Everything in A, **plus** processing/wet-mill capex, warehouse, working capital for regional purchasing, and a coffee fund.
- Processing reference point: GCC's ~$35M wet mill is enterprise-scale; a right-sized mill+warehouse is plausibly **~$1M–$5M**; regional-purchasing working capital another **$0.5M–$2M+**.
- **Indicative total ask: ~$5M–$15M+** (multi-tranche, multi-year). Structure: blended — equity + concessional/mezzanine debt + guarantee/first-loss + TA grant; revolving trade-finance line for purchasing (Mercon/responsAbility analogues).

---

## DECISION IT INFORMS
Whether to pursue Option A or Option B first, the **headline ask number** on the deck, and the **capital structure** (pure equity vs. blended stack) presented to investors.

## PHASE
- **Phase 1:** Option A ask + cost baseline + 2–3 named comparable deals. (Enough to justify a first close.)
- **Phase 2:** Full blended-stack design for Option B, term sheets, and SNSM-specific land/establishment quotes.

## OPTION A vs OPTION B IMPLICATIONS
- **A** maps to patient-debt + modest equity; comparables are Root Capital-scale ($0.2–2M). Lower risk, slower upside, builds operating track record.
- **B** maps to a blended multi-tranche stack and DFI/impact investors (IDB Invest, responsAbility, IFC, Starbucks fund); comparables are GCC-scale ($25–63M) and value-chain credit lines ($50–500M). Higher upside, needs first-loss/guarantee layers and TA to be financeable.

## OPEN UNKNOWNS / RISKS
- **SNSM-specific land $/ha not found** — the single largest input to the ask is unquantified (Phase-2 priority; get local broker/FNC comps). *(Gap)*
- Most cost figures are **2018–2024 and national/LatAm averages**, not SNSM 2026 — inflation and labor-cost rises noted but not quantified. *(Gap)*
- Establishment cost above is **clearing + replant only**; does not fully cost new-planting inputs, shade/agroforestry, or regenerative practices (likely higher). *(Gap)*
- Coffee-price volatility and EUDR compliance affect revenue and financeability. *(Risk)*
- Green Coffee Company's $100M Series C was a *target*, not confirmed as fully raised — treat as ambition, not benchmark. *(Caveat)*
